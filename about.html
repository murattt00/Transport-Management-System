    About Our Company

    This project is a comprehensive web-based management system designed to streamline all operations of a modern logistics company. We integrate customers, containers, vehicles, inventory, finances, and analytics into one intelligent platform.

    Our Project Objectives

    Our mission is to optimize every aspect of the shipping process. Our key objectives include:

    • Streamlining transportation operations from the moment an order is created until it is delivered.
    • Automating all cost, time, and distance calculations to provide fast and accurate quotes.
    • Optimizing container capacity to reduce empty space, which increases profit and efficiency.
    • Managing the company's fleet of ships and trucks, including all associated travel expenses.
    • Tracking the company's financial performance, including revenue, expenses, tax, and profit.
    • Monitoring and controlling product inventory, such as blueberries, by category.
    • Providing detailed analytics and comprehensive company reports for better business insights.
